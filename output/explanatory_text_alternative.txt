Line chart showing the percentage of patients meeting the 150-minute weekly exercise goal (%) on the y-axis across follow-up visits 1 to 4 on the x-axis for two programs. Home-based rehab rises from 58% at visit 1 to 70% at visit 4, while Center-based rehab rises from 57% at visit 1 to 79% at visit 4, leaving a 9-point gap (79% against 70%) at visit 4. Because patients chose their own program and each visit covers only about 45 returning patients per program, this gap is descriptive and could reflect self-selection, dropout, or chance rather than a causal effect.
